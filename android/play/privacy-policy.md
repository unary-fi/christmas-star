# Christmas Star – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 18 November 2026*

Christmas Star is an app for controlling a Christmas Star decoration over
Bluetooth. This policy explains what the app does with information on your
phone.

## The short version

The app collects no personal data and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.
It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not use the
internet at all.

## What the app uses, and why

**Bluetooth.** The app uses Bluetooth to find nearby Christmas Stars and to
send them colours, effects, pictures and settings. On Android 12 and newer,
it scans with the "nearby devices" permission, which does not use your
location. On Android 9 to 11, the system requires the location permission
for any Bluetooth scan; the app uses it only for that and never reads your
location.

**Photos and videos.** When you choose a photo or video, Android's photo
picker gives the app access to that item only. The app works out the colours
under the star's lights on your phone and sends only those colours to the
star, and, if you choose to save a video, an animation made of those colours.
The picture itself never leaves your phone.

**Stored on your phone.** The app stores, in its private storage:
- a random key that pairs your phone with your star,
- which star it is paired with, and your colour and display settings,
- short preview animations of the star's effects, received from the star.

Android may include the pairing key and settings in your device backup
(Google's encrypted backup), so a new phone can control your star. The
optional developer key and the preview animations are never backed up.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. The only device the app talks to is your
Christmas Star.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.
